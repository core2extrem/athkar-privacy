Your rights Policy updates Contact Welcome to Athkar Daily. This policy explains how the app handles your data. The app is built to collect as little data as possible: we operate no server of our own, we never ask for your name or email, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers. One-line summary All your settings and progress are kept only on your phone. The app calls a small set of public services to fetch prayer times, nearby mosques, and your city name — every one of them is listed below. Data kept only on your phone The following is stored locally on your device (inside the app's private storage) and never reaches us or any server we own: • Your theme, font and language choices • Counter progress and the athkar you've completed • Cached prayer times • Cached nearby-mosque lists • Subscription / purchase status (managed via RevenueCat) Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of this data. Third-party services the app calls To provide features like prayer times and Nearest Mosque, the app contacts the services below. Whenever it sends your coordinates, it does so in direct response to the feature you're using — there is no continuous location tracking: Service What is sent Purpose AlAdhan api.aladhan.com Coordinates + chosen calculation method Fetch prayer times for your location BigDataCloud api.bigdatacloud.net Coordinates Translate coordinates into a city / country name for the location pill TomTom Search api.tomtom.com Coordinates + search radius Primary source for nearby-mosque search (provides accurate mosque names in Arabic and English) OpenStreetMap Overpass overpass-api.de Coordinates + search radius Secondary mosque source — used only when TomTom returns no results or is unreachable OpenStreetMap Nominatim nominatim.openstreetmap.org Coordinates Get a nearest-road / district name as a hint for mosques that have no name on OpenStreetMap Masjid Near Me api.masjidnear.me Coordinates + search radius Third-tier fallback mosque source — used only when both TomTom and OpenStreetMap return no results Google Maps google.com/maps Destination coordinates + mosque name Open driving directions inside an in-app browser when you tap a mosque Each service is governed by its own privacy policy: • AlAdhan: aladhan.com/privacy • BigDataCloud: bigdatacloud.com/privacy-and-cookie-policy • TomTom: tomtom.com/privacy • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Overpass + Nominatim): osmfoundation.org Privacy Policy • Masjid Near Me: masjidnear.me • Google Maps: policies.google.com/privacy Purchases & subscriptions All purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing, and the app uses RevenueCat to verify entitlement and manage premium features. When you purchase, your anonymous Play Store user ID and subscription state are shared with these two services only — we never receive your credit-card number or any other payment information. • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy Children's privacy The app is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The app contains no advertising. Your rights and controls Because all your settings stay on your phone, you are in full control: • Clear app data: Settings → Apps → Athkar Daily → Storage → Clear data. • Stop sharing location entirely: Settings → Apps → Athkar Daily → Permissions → Location. • Delete the app and all of its data: simply uninstall. Updates to this policy We may update this policy from time to time when the app gains new features or the services it talks to change. Updates will appear on this page; the "Last updated" date at the top of the page is your indicator.
